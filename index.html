Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in the $120 Standard Clean? ▾

Our $120 Standard Clean covers full home maintenance: dusting all surfaces, sweeping and mopping hard floors, vacuuming carpets and rugs, sanitizing kitchen countertops, exterior appliance wipe-downs, and deep bathroom disinfection including tubs, sinks, and mirrors.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies and equipment? ▾

Yes, absolutely! We bring our own professional-grade supplies, fresh microfiber towels, and cleaning equipment. If you have preferred products or allergies, we are always happy to accommodate your personal preferences!

How do RV and Camper cleans work? ▾

RV cleaning is customized according to the length of your rig (travel trailer, fifth wheel, or motorhome) and whether you need an interior sanitization, exterior wash, or pre/post-camping scrub. Rates start at $140. Simply reach out with your camper model for an exact quote!

How do I pay and how do I schedule? ▾

Scheduling is easy! You can submit our quick website form, text/call Ainslee, or send a message directly via our Facebook Page. We accept cash, check, and convenient digital payments upon completion.
